🔥 In-house engraving guidance

  • Highest contrast: natural / clear-finish maple, beech or birch — the laser burns a crisp dark mark into light wood.
  • Badrutt’s look: medium/warm tones (walnut, butterscotch) give an understated tonal burn — elegant, not loud.
  • Avoid heavy gloss-poly or painted coatings (can melt/discolor). Satin lacquer or natural is ideal — all picks qualify.
  • Placement: the flat front shoulder face, ~1.5–2″ logo — exactly where Badrutt’s sets its crest.
  • Prototype first: the 3-pack Deluxe is the perfect test batch to lock laser power/speed before a full run.

✅ Suggested “prove-the-concept” order

  • 1 × OnlyHangers Deluxe Walnut 3-pack — $38.95
  • 1 × Butler Luxury single (to feel heirloom weight & compare) — ~$27–38
  • Engrave a Poco Loco crest on one of each → pick the winner.
  • Then scale the winner to ~9–12 for one closet.

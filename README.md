# Poco Loco Ranch — OS&E Sourcing Dashboard

Low-volume, brandable, luxury-feel guest supplies for the **Poco Loco Ranch** (Boerne, TX),
inspired by the OS&E at **Badrutt's Palace**. Branding is done **in-house** (laser engraving
for the hanger; embroidery / UVDTF planned for later items).

## How to view

Open **`index.html`** in any browser (double-click it). It's self-contained — all product
photos live in `assets/img/`, no internet required to view.

## Item 01 — Thick, engraveable wood hanger ✅

Sourced against your four constraints: **brandable** (smooth wood face), **low minimum order**,
**price fit** (low cost preferred, quality first), and **currently available** (US in-stock, no
overseas MOQ). Seven options vetted; three featured.

| Tier | Product | Supplier | Per unit | Min order |
|------|---------|----------|----------|-----------|
| ★ **Recommended** | Contoured Deluxe Suit Hanger w/ Non-Slip Bar — Walnut | OnlyHangers | **≈ $12.98** | 3 ( $38.95 / 3-pack ) |
| Premium | Tailor Made® Coat Hanger — Butterscotch + Brass | Butler Luxury | $38.00 | 1 |
| Value | Contoured Suit Hanger w/ Locking Bar — Natural | OnlyHangers | ≈ $2.44 | 25 ( $60.95 / box ) |
| Runner-up | Luxury Wooden Suit Hanger | KirbyAllison | $34.95 | 1 |
| Runner-up | Natural Royal Heirloom (Maple) | Royal Hangers | $1.92 | 25 |
| Runner-up | Contoured Deluxe — Natural | Everything Hangers | $22.95 | 3 |
| Runner-up | Coat Hanger (standard) | Butler Luxury | $27.00 | 1 |

**Why the Walnut Deluxe is the pick:** genuinely thick & heavy (so it won't walk off), warm
walnut tone fits the Hill-Country palette, beech engraves to the same understated burn as the
Badrutt's crest, and the 3-pack minimum is perfect for dialing in your laser before scaling.

**Suggested prove-the-concept order:** one OnlyHangers Deluxe 3-pack ($38.95) + one Butler
Luxury single (~$27–38) to compare heft, engrave a crest on each, then scale the winner to
~9–12 for one closet. *Test spend ≈ $66–77.*

## Methodology / sourcing notes

- Prices, minimums, specs, and the product photos were taken **directly from each supplier's
  live product page on 2026-06-21**. Retail pricing can change — confirm at checkout.
- All featured suppliers ship from **US stock** with **no overseas minimum order**, so you can
  buy in genuinely low volume.
- Photos are each supplier's own product image, downloaded so the dashboard never shows a
  broken link.
